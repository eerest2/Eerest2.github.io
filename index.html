E-Waste Recycling Policy

We accept select electronics, older retro tech, and batteries for responsible recycling and disposal.

To keep our operations running smoothly, we accept specific items—including vintage gear from the '80s, '90s, and 2000s and up. Please check our guidelines below before dropping off equipment.

✅ What We ACCEPT

  • Desktop Computers, Laptops & Retro Gear ('80s, '90s, 2000s+)
  • Smartphones, Tablets & Cell Phones
  • Batteries (Rechargeable & Standard)
  • Circuit Boards, Hard Drives & Electronic Components

❌ What We DO NOT Take

  • CRTs / Old Tube TVs or Monitors
  • Large Household Appliances
  • Printers & Ink Cartridges
  • Unlabeled / Leaking Hazardous Waste Material
